About Us FORTUNE TILES & PAVERS

Fortune Tiles & Pavers is Vadodara's trusted manufacturer of premium cement paving products, serving residential, commercial and industrial projects across Gujarat. Operating from our factory in Dashrath, we offer a complete range of paving solutions — from smooth rubbermould decorative pavers to heavy-duty vibro compaction blocks engineered for high-load applications.

Our manufacturing utilises Apollo Zenith German-designed vibro compaction machinery, producing pavers with compressive strength of ≥ 35 N/mm² that meet and exceed IS:15658 standards. Every product undergoes a mandatory 28-day water curing period before dispatch, ensuring full strength, dimensional accuracy and long service life.

With 13+ product variants available in 6 colour options — Grey, Red, Yellow, Black, Brown and Charcoal — we provide everything needed for walkways, driveways, courtyards, plazas, boundary walls and eco-friendly green spaces.
